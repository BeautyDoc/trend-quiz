About this foundation build

This page is step one of the supply & demand zones tool: real candles in, chart drawn. Two doors, one chart — type a symbol and look it up, or import a CSV of candles (from thinkorswim or anywhere else). CSV imports of daily candles can be viewed as Weekly or Monthly too; the page builds those bars from the dailies.

Relay contract (for the lookup door): the relay receives ?symbol=AAPL&tf=1wk and returns Polygon aggregates JSON unchanged; the relay holds the Polygon key, sets the date window (free plan: 2 years of history), and caches each answer for about 12 hours — end-of-day data only changes once a day, so a whole class can share the free plan's allowance. When your Cloudflare relay goes live, paste its address above and lookup works for any stock or ETF, and for major currency pairs too (type EURUSD).

Data honesty: free and end-of-day feeds are delayed — perfect for zones, which are drawn on closed candles. Prices are as the provider reports them; nothing here is a recommendation.
