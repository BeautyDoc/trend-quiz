Exploding Candle

Load a symbol, then click any candle: a small menu offers what that candle can do — open it up into the smaller-timeframe candles inside it, or draw its supply / demand zone over the chart.

Click a candle and a small menu appears with the actions that fit that candle. Drill down one level opens it into the candles of the next timeframe down (Monthly → Weekly → Daily), right where it sits, with its neighbours still around it. Drill down two levels (Monthly candles only) opens it straight through to its Daily candles in one step. Two levels below the starting timeframe is the deepest the drill goes — the way Mr. Black drills. A candle inside an opened family gets its own menu, including Unexplode to fold its family back up (a family's header strip and its ✕ do the same). Daily candles can't be drilled into — that would need intraday data, which the free feed doesn't include; the menu says so.

Draw zone (in the same menu) draws the candle's zone the Essentials way — a demand zone runs from the bottom of the candle's lowest wick to the top of its body, a supply zone from the top of its highest wick to the bottom of its body — and whether it is demand or supply follows the departure: if price leaves upward it is demand, downward it is supply. Zones stay on the chart while you explode and fold candles, so you can see a zone against the smaller candles inside it. Zones can be drawn from candles inside an exploded family too (the label names that candle's timeframe). A candle that already has a zone offers Remove zone instead; Clear zones takes them all off.

Which candles count as a base? A base candle is a balance candle — body no more than half of its wick-to-wick range — sitting between an impulse in and an impulse out, where buyers and sellers actually fought. A small candle that is only a pause inside one big impulsive leg is not a base, even when its body passes the 50% test. And the deciding check, confirmed by Trade Phantoms Support: drill down one timeframe — if the candles inside it are impulsive, it is not a base on this timeframe; a real base shows balance before one side takes control. Bases that form closing inside an older zone are a retest of that zone; bases that close outside it start a fresh zone.

Growing a zone: a base can span several candles. Click a candle right next to an existing zone and the menu offers Add this candle to the zone — the zone's edges then recompute from every candle in the group, using the most extreme wick and the most extreme body in the group. Click a candle inside a multi-candle zone to Remove this candle from the zone (the edges recompute from what is left) or to remove the whole zone. A zone can hold at most 7 candles — Mr. Black's limit; the menu says so when a zone is full. The label shows the candle count when a zone has more than one.

Two zones side by side — your choice: clicking an unzoned candle next to a zone always offers both Draw zone (its own separate zone) and Add this candle to the zone — nothing ever merges on its own. Separate zones may overlap on screen; that is fine. If two neighbouring zones are the same type (both demand or both supply), a candle in one of them offers Merge with the neighbouring zone, combining them into one zone whose edges are the extremes across all their candles — as long as the combined count stays within the 7-candle limit (the menu notes it when a merge would pass the limit). Neighbouring zones of opposite types never merge; they simply coexist.

Zones travel across timeframes: zones stay on the chart when you switch between Monthly, Weekly and Daily — only loading a new symbol clears them. A zone's prices are the same in every view, its band starts at the candle (or the higher-timeframe candle) that contains where it was drawn, and its label names the timeframe it came from, so a Weekly zone inside a Monthly chart still reads "Weekly". The Zones selector chooses between All timeframes — every timeframe's zones together, for the multiple-timeframe correlation Mr. Black emphasises — and This timeframe, which shows only the current timeframe's zones and merely hides the rest (switch back and they return). A zone carried over from another timeframe is for looking at in this view; adding candles, merging and removing still happen on its own timeframe. Clear zones removes all of them, whichever the selector says.

Broken zones: zones that price has closed through are shown hatched with dashed borders and marked BROKEN in the label. Only a candle close through the zone breaks it — below a demand zone's bottom edge, or above a supply zone's top edge — and that close must be on the zone's own timeframe or a higher one: a Weekly zone can be broken by a Weekly or Monthly close, never by a Daily one, and lower timeframes cannot break a zone. A wick through the edge doesn't count, and a still-forming higher-timeframe candle hasn't closed yet, so it can't break a zone either. A broken zone keeps its name and colour: a broken demand zone does not become a supply zone.

Show gaps (the toggle above) marks the empty price space between one candle's close and the next candle's open wherever the two don't meet — the mark sits between the two candles, in the space between their bodies, coloured by direction: green for an up gap, red for a down gap, the way Mr. Black draws them — and it is display only: it changes no data, no zones and no drilling.

Crosshair & scaling: move your pointer over the chart — the vertical line sits on the hovered candle, the horizontal line follows your pointer, and the blue tag on the right price axis reads the exact price at that line. When you click a candle, its marker stays pinned to that candle while the menu is open, so you can still see which candle the menu belongs to while you choose. Drag the price axis (the numbers on the right) up or down to stretch or squish the chart vertically — the stretch pivots on the middle of the candles on screen, so tops and bottoms spread evenly; drag the date axis (the numbers along the bottom) left or right to spread or squeeze the candles — the chart stays jammed up against the most recent candles on the right, so spreading and squeezing happen from the left side only, squeezing stops once all the candles are shown, and spreading can go all the way down to just a few candles left on the chart. Double-click either axis to reset just that axis; Reset view resets both. Once you have spread or stretched the chart, drag on the chart itself to slide it — left and right through the candles, up and down through the prices — and bring the area you care about back into view; a plain click with no drag still opens a candle's menu, exactly as before.

Copy chart image puts a picture of the chart exactly as it looks right now on your clipboard to paste into an email (or downloads it if the browser refuses) — handy for sending a chart to Trade Phantoms Support.

Data: choose the lookup source above — your relay (Polygon daily candles, about two years) or Twelve Data with your own free key (kept only in this browser, and shared with the Zone Chart on this site, so a key pasted there is already here). Weekly and Monthly are built from the daily candles in the page, exactly as on the Zone Chart.
